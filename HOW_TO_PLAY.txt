HOW TO PLAY - THE SURVIVAL CIV GAME (build b13)
==============================================

INSTALLING WITHOUT PYTHON (Windows players):
- Download Civilization-Setup-b13.exe from the releases page and run it: it
  installs the game, makes a desktop / start menu shortcut and can be removed
  from "Apps & features" like any other program.
- The installer can also put the dedicated server (CivilizationServer.exe) next
  to the game - pick "Сервер для друзей" during setup if a friend will host.
- With Python installed you can use installer/install.py instead (any system).

REQUIREMENTS:
1. Python 3.10 or newer: https://www.python.org/downloads/
2. Then install pygame once:   pip install -r requirements.txt

LANGUAGES: English / Polski / Русский - press F2 in the main menu, or pick it in
SETTINGS (also inside the game: Esc -> Settings).

PLAYING WITH FRIENDS - THE SERVER IS ITS OWN DOWNLOAD NOW (b12):
- The server no longer needs pygame and ships as Civilization_Server_<build>.zip:
  unzip it anywhere and run "python run_server.py" (or CivilizationServer.exe).
- Check it first: python check_server.py --forbid-pygame  -> "the server works".
- Give your friends the address printed at startup: <your-ip>:<port>.
- Updates: the launcher follows a channel - stable (real releases) or beta (every
  bNN build) - and keeps the three previous versions for the "Roll back" button.

HOW TO RUN (HOST / SERVER):
1. One person must be the HOST.
2. Open the game folder in a terminal.
3. Run:  python run_server.py
4. The window shows the address to share, e.g. 192.168.1.5:5555
5. Give that address to your friends (same Wi-Fi / LAN).
6. If friends cannot connect, allow Python through the Windows Firewall
   (Windows asks the first time you start the server - choose "Private networks").
7. The world is saved to saves/world.json every minute, when the server stops
   (Ctrl+C) and when somebody types /save in the chat.
   Use  python run_server.py --save saves/friends.json  to keep several worlds,
   --no-save for a disposable game, or --no-accounts to switch accounts off.

HOW TO RUN (PLAYER):
1. Open the game folder.
2. Double-click launcher.py, or run:  python run_client.py
3. Type the host address (127.0.0.1 for a game on your own PC).
4. Either CREATE ACCOUNT + LOG IN (your inventory, HP and position come back the
   next time you log in), or PLAY AS GUEST (nothing is kept between sessions).
5. Tab moves between the fields, ESC quits the menu.

THE LAUNCHER (optional):
- launcher.py -> PLAY starts the game, Check updates downloads the newest build.
- Updates come from GitHub (the newest release, or the repository archive itself).
- Before installing, the launcher copies your folder to backup_before_update_...
- Builds are named with letters and digits: b8, b9 ... b10 (b10 is newer than b9).

CONTROLS:
  WASD ............ walk
  Left mouse ...... gather / punch a player under the cursor
  Right mouse ..... open the building under the cursor
  1..9, wheel ..... take an item from the hotbar into your hand
  E ............... gather the nearest resource / harvest a farm
  C ............... crafting and buildings
  I ............... inventory (click an item to hold it, right-click to drop it)
  V ............... cities and countries (roles, tax, fund, diplomacy)
  Q ............... eat the best food you carry (or right-click food)
  H ............... use a medkit or a bandage
  B ............... crafting menu, opened on the Buildings tab
  Y ............... market: offers and your own trade
  F ............... use a building (town center, research table, furnace)
  Enter ........... chat (/help /save /wipe /regen)
  P ............... place a waypoint, Shift+P removes the nearest one
  O ............... sort the inventory, Shift+O fills the hotbar
  F1 .............. help: first steps, keys, world, cities
  Tab (hold) ...... player list
  Esc ............. pause / close a menu
  F11 ............. fullscreen

  (every key can be changed: pause -> Settings -> Keys)

CRAFTING AND BUILDING:
- Crafting needs the right station: tools and swords at the crafting table,
  iron tools / pistol at the advanced workbench, gunpowder at the chemical plant.
- In the crafting menu: left click = craft 1, right click = craft 5,
  Shift + left click = craft as many as you can.
- Buildings tab: pick a building, then click a tile within 8 tiles of yourself.
- Everything you do depends on the item in your hand: an axe fells trees faster,
  a sword hits harder, a pistol needs ammo. Empty hands are weak.

RESOURCE PACKS:
- Packs live in resourcepacks/<name>/ (pack.json + textures/*.png). Pick one in
  SETTINGS; missing textures fall back to the built-in ones.
- Make your own:  python tools/make_resourcepack.py --all

TIPS:
- The server is authoritative: if you lag, your position snaps back.
- Progress is stored in server_data/accounts.json (PBKDF2-hashed passwords).
- Two people cannot log into the same account at once.

NEW IN b13 - BIOMES, SEASONS, MUSIC, FIRST STEPS AND COMFORT
- The land is not one big field any more: meadow, forest, desert, snowy tundra,
  swamp and caves. Trees grow in forests, ore hides in caves, sheep like meadows,
  wolves and bears prefer forests and tundra. Swamps and tundra slow you down
  (0.8 / 0.85), sand is a little quicker (1.05) - the client uses the same
  numbers as the server, so nothing rubber-bands.
- SEASONS: spring -> summer -> autumn -> winter, a few game days each. Spring
  grows the woods back, autumn fills the world with mushrooms and berries,
  winter slows everybody down and turns the weather to fog. The day number and
  the season are next to the clock; snow falls in winter. A server can switch
  the whole thing off with "seasons": false in server_config.json.
- MUSIC: calm day and night tracks, separate music in caves and in a fight.
  Put your own files into assets/music/ (or a resource pack) - see "YOUR OWN
  MUSIC" below. Music on/off and its volume are in the settings.
- FIRST STEPS: a chain of ten small goals for a new player (look around, get
  wood, make a tool, stone, a workbench, a campfire, eat, a torch, found a city,
  survive your first winter). The current step is shown in the corner, the
  progress is saved in config.json, and Settings has "Start the first steps
  over". Press F1 any time for help: first steps, keys, world, cities.
- YOUR KEYS, YOUR RULES: pause -> Settings -> Keys. Click a line and press the
  key you want; if the key is taken the game tells you who has it and offers to
  swap the two actions. "Reset the keys" brings the defaults back.
- WAYPOINTS: P places a mark where you stand, Shift+P removes the nearest one.
  Marks show up on the minimap and as an arrow with the distance in the corner.
  They are kept per server in waypoints.json.
- INVENTORY: O switches the order (category -> amount -> name), Shift+O drops
  your tools and food into the hotbar.
- CHAT CHANNELS: /g everybody, /l only people nearby (12 tiles), /c your country,
  /w <name> <text> one person. A bare /g, /l or /c switches the channel you use
  from then on (remembered in the settings).

NEW IN b11 - WALKING ON WATER AND A REAL CHARACTER
- You can now walk INTO the water: you wade through it at 40% speed (server key
  "swim_speed"). You never drown and you can always climb back to the shore.
  A bridge over the water is still the fast way across (1.2x), a road is 1.35x,
  and rock blocks everybody. Set "swim": false to get the old b9 rule back.
- The player is a little PERSON now, not a coloured cube: head, body, arms and
  legs, your own colour, he turns the way you walk and leaves ripples in water.
- The inventory no longer shows the same stack twice (in the grid and in the
  hotbar row) - the grid lists what the hotbar does not show yet.
- Bridges can be placed on the water now. The screen used to say "you cannot
  build here" on a river: the client thought water blocks everything, the
  server was happy. Both sides share one rule again (rock never, a bridge only
  on water, everything else only on dry land).
- Nobody can build THROUGH the city walls any more: an outsider used to stand
  just outside the walls and drop a tower inside them. Every tile of the
  building is checked now, and the message names the city that refused.
- Sounds: put your own files (wav / ogg / mp3) into `assets/sounds/` or into
  `resourcepacks/<your pack>/sounds/` and pick the pack in the settings - see
  "YOUR OWN SOUNDS" below.
- Hungry? Press Q to eat the best food you carry, or right-click a food item in
  the inventory.

YOUR OWN SOUNDS:
  1. Name your files after the game's sounds (chop, mine, hit, hurt, shot, bow,
     craft, build, break, eat, step, pickup, task, death, night, rain) - the
     extension may be .wav, .ogg or .mp3.
  2. Either drop them into assets/sounds/ (they replace the built-in ones), or
     make a resource pack: resourcepacks/mysounds/sounds/*.ogg plus
     resourcepacks/mysounds/pack.json with {"name": "My sounds"}.
  3. Pick that pack in SETTINGS inside the game - the new sounds apply at once.
  The built-in sounds are generated by:  python tools/make_sounds.py --force

YOUR OWN MUSIC:
- Music works like the sounds: resource pack first, then assets/music/. The file
  name says when it plays - day_*.ogg, night_*.ogg, cave_*.ogg, battle_*.ogg,
  calm_*.ogg (calm is the fallback). .ogg, .mp3 and .wav all work.
- The two built-in tracks are short loops made by the game itself:
  python tools/make_sounds.py --force   # regenerates sounds and music

NEW IN b10 - CIVILISATION
- Roles: a city leader (V menu, click a member, or /role <name> <role>) gives
  out ELDER, BUILDER, WARRIOR or CITIZEN. Inside the city walls only builders and
  elders may build and use the storage, only elders research; the warrior gets
  +10% melee damage. Outside the walls everybody is as free as before.
- TAX: the leader sets 0-40% (-/+ buttons in the V menu or /tax 40). The tax is
  taken from what members gather INSIDE the city and goes into the city fund;
  the fund pays for the buildings of that city first, and only then your own
  pack. /city shows the tier, the tax, the fund and the roles.
- SIEGE: a town centre can only be broken during a war, and the city is warned
  while it is attacked. When it falls, a country takes the city over: the old
  leader is thrown out, the posts are vacant and the fund goes to the winner.
  Without a country you can only SACK the city (fund + one tier down).
- WINNING: hold every city on the map, or build a WONDER (3x3, 2000 HP, 400
  wood + 300 stone + 150 iron + 100 gold, needs industrialization and the
  industrial age, only a city leader may build it). The winner is announced to
  everybody and shown on screen.
- AGES: stone -> bronze -> iron -> industrial -> electric, following the techs
  of your country (the age is shown in the HUD and in the V menu).
- MEDICINE (tech): hospital, bandage (15 HP) and medkit (45 HP + cures
  poisoning). Press H to use what you carry; a hospital heals everybody within
  140 px for 6 HP every 5 seconds.
- ELECTRICITY (tech): generator and lamp. A lamp only shines within 400 px of a
  generator - without power it stays dark.

NEW IN b9 - THE LIVING WORLD
- Water: five lakes and a river cut the map. You cannot swim, so build a BRIDGE
  (4 wood, masonry) on the water to cross it. The minimap shows water in blue.
- Caves (seven of them, one entrance each) are dark inside - take a torch. They
  grow mushrooms and hide deep veins of iron, gold, coal and sulfur: mining in a
  cave pays DOUBLE. Bears live down there.
- Fishing: craft a fishing rod (3 wood + 2 string, agriculture), stand within two
  tiles of water and press E. You catch 1-2 fish; cook them on a campfire into
  cooked fish. Mushrooms are edible but slightly poisonous.
- Weather is now gameplay: rain slows walking down and doubles the speed of farm
  growth, fog dims torches and campfires. Turn it off with "weather": false.
- The wolves now hunt in packs of 3-5 and take revenge when one of them is hurt.
- Countries can be at war, at peace (default) or allied. The leader picks a
  country with the right mouse button in the V menu and presses War / Peace /
  Alliance (or uses /war /peace /ally <country> in the chat). While two countries
  are at peace nobody can hurt their players or buildings.
- The MARKET (60 wood + 20 stone, logistics) opens with Y when you stand next to
  it: post "I give X for Y" and take other people's offers. The goods of an offer
  are held by the market; the payment goes to the seller's inventory, to his
  city storage, or waits for him to log in again.

NEW IN b8 - SURVIVAL, WEAR AND ANIMALS
- Food matters: hunger falls over time, you starve at 0 and slowly heal when
  well fed. Press Q to eat the best food you carry, or use the inventory
  (right click food). Raw meat can make you sick.
- Tools wear out. The bar under an item shows what is left; a broken tool
  disappears from your hand.
- Armour: helmet, chest, legs, feet. Right click an armour piece to wear it,
  click an armour slot to take it off. Armour cuts incoming damage (up to 75%).
- Animals wander the world: sheep, cows, chickens and wolves. Hit them for
  meat, leather, wool and string; right click one with raw meat in the pack to
  tame it. Wolves hunt at night - stay near a torch or a campfire.
- Day and night cycle plus weather (clear / rain / fog) with light from torches
  and campfires. Night is dark, so place lights before dusk.
- Buildings have health: hit a foreign wall to break it, repair your own with R
  (or right click it) - stone and wood are spent.
- Chests and warehouses store items; press E near them (right click) to open the
  storage screen: left click = 1, right click = all, Shift = whole stack.
- Tasks and achievements: press K to see them; rewards land in your inventory.
- Research: city tiers (town centre) and country technologies (research table)
  unlock new buildings and recipes. Only leaders can research.
- Chat commands: /who /online /where /time /day /msg <name> <text>.
- b10 adds /role <name> <role>, /tax <0-40> and /city.
- Press M for the minimap (terrain, buildings, cities, players, pets).
- Sound effects: generated by tools/make_sounds.py; volume and on/off are in the
  settings screen.
- Server settings live in server_config.json: pvp, day_length, hunger_rate,
  resource_rate, durability, weather, swim, swim_speed, animals, wolves,
  animal_respawn, taxes, victory, start_kit, max_players, save_interval.
  Command line: --config <file>.
